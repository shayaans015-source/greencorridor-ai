System Architecture & Preemption FAQ
Technical Overview

1. Why Simple Green-Switch Fails in High Traffic

Switching a signal to green the moment an ambulance reaches an intersection fails if 6–10 civilian vehicles are already stopped ahead of it. The ambulance remains trapped behind stationary traffic.

Solution: Clears queued vehicles 6–12 seconds before ambulance arrival

2. Predictive Queue-Clearance Lead Time

When an emergency vehicle is detected at 240 meters, the controller calculates its arrival time and triggers green early enough so the last queued car crosses the stop-line right before the ambulance arrives.

Result: Non-stop 55 km/h green wave through the intersection

3. Cross-Traffic Safety Clearance

Preemption never snaps directly from cross-street green to conflicting red. The controller enforces a mandatory 3-second amber and all-red clearance window to prevent intersection collisions.

Safety Standard: 2.0s Amber + 1.0s All-Red Buffer

4. Post-Preemption Traffic Recovery

Once the emergency vehicle clears the intersection box, the system automatically allocates compensatory green time to interrupted approaches so cross-street commuters recover smoothly.

Fairness: Restores normal commuter flow within 1.5 cycles
